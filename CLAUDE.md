# Agent Instructions

The rules for this repository live in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Read that file first, follow it exactly, and do not duplicate its rules here.

Installing for someone who has never seen this project? Do not dictate commands: walk [docs/ONBOARDING-CHAT.md](docs/ONBOARDING-CHAT.md) with them in chat, in their language (`docs/ONBOARDING-CHAT.ru.md`, `docs/ONBOARDING-CHAT.zh.md`), one question at a time.

Before grepping this tree, ask its knowledge graph: `graphify-out/graph.json` and `graphify-out/GRAPH_REPORT.md` are tracked and free to walk.
